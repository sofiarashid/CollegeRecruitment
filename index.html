The College of New Jersey

Located in Ewing, New Jersey, TCNJ is a public four-year college known for a liberal arts foundation, close faculty mentorship, and career-focused learning. Its campus combines the feel of a small college with the opportunities of a major university region.

What makes TCNJ unique: students get a 13:1 student-to-faculty ratio and more than 200 organizations to help connect classroom learning with community.
